# Scale MBS — Services

Scale MBS offers **full-suite web and system development**. Core service lines:

## 1. Website Design & Development
- Custom business websites and e-commerce builds (Webflow and custom development).
- ADA compliance for websites.
- Nationwide delivery — remote-first, serving clients across the U.S.

## 2. CRM Implementation & Business Systems
- CRM design and rollout (Scale runs its own operation on Monday.com — CRM + dev/sprint products — and implements similar systems for clients).
- Pipeline design: leads, deals, accounts, contacts, and client-project tracking.
- Standard operating procedures (SOPs), training material, and workflow automations.

## 3. AI Automation & Smart Integrations
- AI-powered automation for repetitive business processes.
- Smart technology integrations that connect a client's existing tools (email, calendar, CRM, e-commerce platforms) into one efficient system.
- AI assistants/chatbots and email-management tooling (e.g., the in-house Fyxer-style email assistant project).

## 4. E-commerce & Tech Support
- E-commerce setup and optimization.
- Tailored, ongoing tech support for small businesses.

## Proposal & Delivery Process
- New client work typically starts with a written proposal (e.g., the "COGO City Proposal — Scale MBS", October 2025) outlining the plan to design, develop, and deliver the system.
- Client onboarding is supported by a branded onboarding presentation covering: Introduction, About Us, Your Product, The Plans, and Our Solution.
- Active client work is tracked on the "Client Projects" and "Websites" boards in Monday.com; internal dev work runs in sprints.
